These are searchable transcriptions of **all 19 PDFs (449 pages) and the one DOCX** found in the repository. Original documents and Jupyter notebooks are unchanged. No additional PDF or DOCX files were found in subdirectories.

## Reading and citing

- Each PDF page has a consecutive `## Slide N` or `## Page N` heading, including title and closing pages. Numbers refer to **physical PDF pages**, not lecture numbers or numbers printed inside a slide.
- Cite a Markdown filename and its slide/page heading. Follow the source link when you need the exact appearance of a figure.
- Bullet nesting, numbered procedures, worked values, tables, and code are transcribed rather than summarized. Formulas use plain text or LaTeX. Repeated copyright/footer text and printed page numbers are omitted.
- Diagram labels are included where recoverable. `[Figure: ...]` describes visual content that cannot be faithfully represented as text; it is not a replacement image.
- The DOCX is organized by its four original questions. Its metadata reports three pages, but automatic Word pagination is not reliably recoverable without the original rendering environment.

## Conversion and quality checks

Text was extracted locally with Poppler/PyMuPDF; Word paragraphs, tables, and content-control text were read with python-docx and XML inspection. Tesseract OCR was used for embedded screenshots, diagram labels, and image-only worked examples. No documents were uploaded to an external conversion service.

The transcriptions are study/search aids, not corrected editions. They preserve apparent mistakes in the source; explicitly flagged uncertain OCR should not be used as a reliable numerical answer. Graph geometry, color, arrow routing, and tiny screenshot text can be lossy even when their surrounding explanation is complete. Use the linked original for those details.
